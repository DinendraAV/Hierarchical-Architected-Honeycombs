# Audit of the IAC 2025 paper (to complete)

Paper: "Design, Analysis and Comparison of Biomimetic Hierarchical Honeycomb Sandwich Composites for Aerospace Applications".

Known loose ends:
- Core masses differed by factors of about 3 to 5.6 between configurations, so hierarchy and mass were confounded.
- HHC-2 geometry was a slotted-plug construction; its relative density values were not reliable.
- Sandwich skins and core were studied together, which blurred the core's own behaviour.
