# Scope (locked 2026-10-18)

- Geometry: RHC against HHC-2, bare core only, at equal relative density, three density levels.
- Why equal relative density: separates the effect of hierarchy from the effect of extra mass
  (the IAC cores differed in mass by factors of about 3 to 5.6).
- Material: printed TPU (Ultimaker TPU 95A is the candidate; Prof. Lomazzi will confirm material and build parameters).
- Tier 1: periodic RVE elastic homogenisation, six load cases per configuration.
- Tier 2: crush at two rates (the paper's contribution). Rates in the experiments: 5 to 500 mm/min.
- Tier 3 (stretch): out-of-plane tension and a core-beam check.
- Solvers: Abaqus/Explicit and LS-DYNA are equally acceptable to the supervisor.
- Targets: ICF16 abstract 30 Nov 2026; experiments from mid-Nov 2026; manuscript April 2027.
- Weekly progress meeting: Tuesdays.
